About ZENDIA

We coordinate the practical arrangements involved in medical visits by working together with hospitals, travel agencies, interpreters, and other related parties.

Japan's medical care is known for its high precision and reliability, drawing interest from patients around the world. At the same time, receiving medical care from overseas requires coordination across many areas, including language, appointments, transportation, accommodation, payment, and communication. ZENDIA works to reduce this burden as much as possible, arranging and coordinating so that patients can receive care in a reassuring environment, and so that medical institutions can maintain a sustainable system for accepting patients under fair compensation.

ZENDIA's role is to connect the practical elements involved in a medical visit — hospitals, accommodation, transportation, and interpretation — and to see the entire process through smoothly. Rather than flash, we value accuracy and sincerity, handling each case steadily and carefully.

Business Overview

  • Advance coordination and practical support for medical visits

  • Medical interpretation and translation services

  • Planning of the overall stay, including transportation and accommodation

  • Communication and progress management among related parties
